# Autumn Colors in Design Systems

When temperatures drop and foliage shifts, nature provides the ideal case study in organic desaturation and tonal warmth.

## Seasonal Hex Palette
- **Ochre Amber**: `#C88D4E` &mdash; Warm highlights and callout badges.
- **Burnt Terracotta**: `#B05B3B` &mdash; Alert and warning signals.
- **Deep Moss**: `#5A6B53` &mdash; Secondary foliage tones.
- **Parchment Mist**: `#EDE8D0` &mdash; Layered card surfaces.

## Harmonizing with Dark Monospace

Pairing these earthy tones with dense JetBrains Mono typography and ink-ruled hairline dividers anchors the seasonal warmth in computational rigor.
